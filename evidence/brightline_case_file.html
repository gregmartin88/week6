EXHIBIT A · SIMULATED

Responsible AI Charter (excerpts)

Source: "Brightline Responsible AI Charter, v1.0," adopted by the Board 12 Sep 2024, published at brightline.example/responsible-ai. Executive sponsor: Chief Product Officer.

"At Brightline, we believe hiring technology should open doors, not close them. These seven principles guide every model we build and every product we sell."
#PrincipleCharter textOperational commitment
1Fairness"We test every model for adverse impact before it reaches a single candidate."Before each production release, adverse impact ratio (AIR) of at least 0.80 (four-fifths rule) across sex, race/ethnicity and age 40+. A failing release is blocked until the Fairness Review Board signs off.
2Transparency"Customers and candidates deserve to know how our models work."A public model card is updated with every material model change, including retraining, within 30 days of release.
3Accountability"Responsibility for AI outcomes sits with named leaders, not with algorithms."The Fairness Review Board (7 members, quorum 5) meets at least quarterly and reports to the Audit Committee twice a year.
4Human Oversight"Our models recommend; people decide."Customers are told the score is advisory. No auto-rejection without customer opt-in.
5Privacy"Candidate data is used only to serve the hiring decision it was provided for."Data minimization and a 24-month retention limit.
6Robustness"We monitor our models in the real world, not only in the lab."Quarterly drift and disparity monitoring on live customer data.
7Listening & Continuous Improvement"Every concern raised is a gift."Any report tagged Fairness is triaged by the RAI Office within 10 business days. A Fairness item may be reclassified only with RAI Office approval, with the reason recorded.

§8, External Communications: "Public and customer-facing claims about the fairness of Brightline products must be reviewed by the RAI Office and Legal before use. Brightline does not claim its products eliminate bias; we claim we measure and mitigate it."

Log statistics (reviewer-computed)

  • 10 tickets were tagged Fairness. One (CS-4230) was a genuine mis-tag, which leaves 9 substantive fairness concerns.
  • Of those 9, 8 were reclassified to a lower-priority, non-fairness type: 5 Feature Request, 1 Collateral Update Request, 1 Known Limitation, 1 Tech Debt. The 9th was closed as "Not Reproducible" within 3 days.
  • None show the RAI Office approval that Principle 7 requires. The audit trail shows no RAI Office user acting on any ticket.
  • None led to a model change. Median priority moved from P2 to P4.
  • 7 of the 9 were raised by the two CSMs who corroborate the whistleblower (R. Okafor: 4; M. Duarte: 3).
  • Both release-blocker tickets from Data Science were downgraded by the same executive, and each time the model shipped within 3–7 days.
